About

I am an astrophysicist interested in compact objects, particularly magnetars and other neutron stars, and the extreme physical processes that make them some of the most fascinating objects in the Universe. I completed my PhD in Astrophysics in October 2024 at the Institute of Space Sciences (ICE-CSIC) and the Autonomous University of Barcelona (UAB), Spain.

My research combines X-ray observations from observatories such as XMM-Newton, NuSTAR, and Swift with radio observations from facilities including VLA and ASKAP. I investigate magnetar outbursts, X-ray bursts, and transient phenomena, as well as the search for pulsars and other variable sources.

Alongside my research, I work with complex scientific datasets and develop computational workflows for data analysis, time-series processing, signal detection, and astronomical visualisation. I am particularly interested in extending these approaches towards broader data science and applied artificial intelligence.

Beyond research, I am committed to strengthening astronomy and physics education and international scientific collaboration. I co-founded and manage Photon Scientific Centre for Natural Sciences and Metaphysics (Photon SCNSM), coordinate initiatives including programmes supported by ICTP's Physics Without Frontiers, contribute to IAU outreach, and develop public-facing astronomy content through Zool Astro.

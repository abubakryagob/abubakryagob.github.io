01 · Primary focus

Astrophysics & Research

Exploring compact objects and high-energy transient phenomena through multiwavelength observations.

Published research · 2023 ApJ

Swift J1818.0−1607

A multiwavelength investigation of the first outburst of a young magnetar, using XMM-Newton and NuSTAR data reduction, broadband spectral analysis across multiple epochs, extended diffuse X-ray emission, and complementary VLA observations.

Read the 2023 ApJ publication →

Published research · 2024 ApJ

SGR J1935+2154

X-ray and radio investigation of the 2022 magnetar reactivation, including XMM-Newton and NuSTAR observations, phase-resolved spectroscopy, NICER timing analysis, and radio burst investigation.

Read the 2024 ApJ publication →

Ongoing research · manuscript in preparation

ZTF J1901+1458

Ongoing investigation of radio emission from an isolated magnetic white dwarf pulsar using ASKAP and VLA archival data, radio imaging, dynamic-spectra analysis, and international collaboration.

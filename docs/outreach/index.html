Outreach & Public Engagement

Bringing astrophysics to people - from children under desert skies to families in Barcelona parks.

Magnet Alliance at ICE-CSIC

2022 – 2024

Member of the Magnet Alliance outreach programme at the Institute of Space Sciences (ICE-CSIC), Barcelona. The Magnet programme, led by Fundacio Bofill, fights school segregation in Catalonia by connecting research institutes with under-resourced public schools. I contributed as an outreach researcher working alongside teachers and students at Gabriel Castella i Raich school in Igualada.

ICE-CSIC Featured Event

Astronomy for Sahrawi Children: Amanar Project, Castelldefels

August 25, 2023

Co-organised an astronomy outreach session for Sahrawi children visiting Spain through the "Vacaciones en Paz" programme, in collaboration with the Castelldefels Astronomical Group (Astrofels). Activities included a Solar System cosmic walk (Earth = peppercorn scale), followed by telescope observation of the Moon and Saturn. Attended by 12 children and their host families.
